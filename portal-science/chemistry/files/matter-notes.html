Matter in Our Surroundings

Course Code: SCI-CHEM-01 | Class 9 Science Academy

1. Physical Nature of Matter

Everything in this universe is made up of material which scientists have named "matter". Matter is anything that occupies space and has mass.

1.1 Characteristics of Particles of Matter

Particle Theory: Particles of matter have spaces between them, they are continuously moving (possessing kinetic energy), and they attract each other with mutual forces.

2. States of Matter

Matter exists primarily in three distinct physical states based on particle characteristics:

  • Solid State: Definite shape, distinct boundaries, and fixed volumes with negligible compressibility.
  • Liquid State: Fixed volume but no fixed shape; takes the shape of the container. Exhibits fluidity.
  • Gaseous State: Highly compressible, no fixed volume or shape, and particles move randomly at high speeds.

3. Evaporation

A phenomenon in which liquid changes into vapor at any temperature below its boiling point is called evaporation. It is a surface phenomenon influenced by surface area, temperature, humidity, and wind speed, resulting in cooling.
