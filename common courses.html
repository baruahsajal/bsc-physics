Unit 1: Locational Significance

Assam covers 78,438 sq km. It is strategically located in the northeastern region of India.

Location: 24°8'N to 28°2'N latitude and 89°42'E to 96°E longitude
  • Position: Bordered by Bhutan and Arunachal Pradesh (North), Nagaland and Manipur (East), Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram and Bangladesh (South), West Bengal (West)
  • Gateway: Acts as the corridor connecting the NE states with the rest of India.

Q: Discuss the locational significance of Assam. (5 marks)

Gateway Position: Acts as the corridor and gateway to the entire northeastern region.
Strategic Location: Shares international borders with Bhutan and Bangladesh.
Biodiversity Hotspot: Located in Indo-Burma biodiversity region.
